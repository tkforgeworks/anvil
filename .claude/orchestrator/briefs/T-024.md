## Goal
Port the ANVL-158 spike's editor shortcut layer into the app on top of the ANVL-159
`RecordEditorController<T>`: Ctrl+S saves the focused editor's draft wherever the cursor is,
Ctrl+Z inside a text field is the field's own undo and outside a field steps the draft, redo
mirrors it (Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y), and the editor's own `FocusScope` keeps the shortcuts
reachable (design §8.7). Jira ANVL-162 (the orchestrator handles Jira; you do not).

## Decisions already taken (do not reopen)
- `lib/app/editor/editor_shortcuts.dart`: `SaveRecordIntent`, `UndoDraftIntent`,
  `RedoDraftIntent`, `textFieldHasFocus()`, the enabled-only-outside-a-field draft action, and
  `RecordEditorShortcuts({onSave, onUndo, onRedo, child})` with the spike's four bindings, ported
  as they are. Keep the layering diagram and the "rejected" note in the library doc.
- `lib/app/editor/record_editor_scope.dart`: `RecordEditorScope<T>` (a `ConsumerWidget` or
  `ConsumerStatefulWidget`) taking the editor's `AsyncNotifierProvider.autoDispose.family`
  instance for one record (`provider`) and a `child`, and composing `RecordEditorShortcuts`
  (wired to `ref.read(provider.notifier).save()` / `undo()` / `redo()`) around a
  `FocusScope(autofocus: true)` whose node has `debugLabel: 'editor'`, owned and disposed by the
  widget. This is the one wrapper every domain editor (ANVL-191 to 197) and the shared scaffold
  (ANVL-187) put around their content. `save()`'s outcome is returned to an optional
  `onSaveOutcome(SaveOutcome)` callback so the scaffold can show its result; no snackbar here.
- Ctrl+S with a clean draft calls `save()` and gets `nothingToSave`: nothing visible happens
  (the ticket's "nothing visible beyond the status").
- Global shortcuts (the ANVL-186 registry) are not built here. The leave guard (`PopScope`,
  ANVL-161), `DraftTextField` and the scaffold widgets (ANVL-187) are not built here.
- The shortcut cheatsheet and settings list (the ticket's last criterion) are ANVL-186/189;
  report it as out of scope.
